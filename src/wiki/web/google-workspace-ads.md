# Google Workspace Ads

At some point, I started seeing large **Upgrade** buttons at the top
of many Google Workspace products on my *personal* account. It took a while to
figure out how to disable these, so I’m documenting it here.

## How to disable Google Workspace upgrade ads on a personal account

As of September 2026, the process is:

1. Give up. ☹

<p>
Sadly, the buttons have returned, and the process below no longer seems to have any effect. I've kept it documented here for posterity. If you find a new way to get rid of these buttons, please
<a href="https://airtable.com/appopNVjvtXgt5gQQ/pagwo1PQB92bHio2c/form?prefill_URL=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.lumeh.org%2Fwiki%2Fweb%2Fgoogle-workspace-ads%2F&prefill_Description=I+have+found+a+way+to+get+rid+of+the+buttons%3A%20&hide_Attachments=true" rel=external target=_blank

<div style="text-decoration: line-through">

1. Click your profile photo in the upper right corner.
2. Click the **Manager your Google Account** button.
3. Click **People & sharing** in the left sidebar.
4. Scroll down to the *Business features* section and click on **Business personalization**.
5. Turn off **Business personalization**.

I originally found this information in a [comment in a Google Support thread].
